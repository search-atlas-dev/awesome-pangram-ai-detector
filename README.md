- [Enterprise](https://www.pangram.com/enterprise) - the "Contact Sales" and "Partner With Us" destination for institutions.
- [Login](https://www.pangram.com/login) - existing accounts.
- [Third-party Pangram evaluations](https://www.pangram.com/blog/third-party-pangram-evals) - the company's own roundup of outside studies it cites for its accuracy claims.

## Getting started

- Text detection - paste text or upload a file in the dashboard; the Medium review below tested ChatGPT, Claude and DeepSeek output this way.
- Image detection - JPG, PNG or WebP, minimum 512 by 512 pixels, up to 30 MB per file, three free scans per day according to the homepage widget.
- AI assistance detection - the homepage lists "Detects AI Assistance"; the Medium review describes it as a Pangram 3.0 feature for mixed-authorship drafts.
- Text Comparison and AI Phrase Detection - two further dashboard features the Medium review walks through side by side.
